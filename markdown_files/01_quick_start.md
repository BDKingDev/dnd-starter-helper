# Rescue One-Shot Character Cards: Quick Start

Use these cards to help brand-new players create a light, playable character in 3–5 minutes.

## Player Decision Flow

1. Pick one **Character/Class Card**.
2. Pick one **Adventuring Drive Card**.
3. Pick one **Someone or Something You Care About Card**.
4. Pick one **Flaw Card**.
5. Answer **one** question from any card you picked.
6. Start playing.

## What Each Choice Means

**Character/Class** = what you do during play.  
**Adventuring Drive** = why you became an adventurer in the first place.  
**Someone or Something You Care About** = one concrete person, place, object, promise, group, pet, rival, or memory that matters to you.  
**Flaw** = how you make things complicated in an interesting but party-friendly way.

## Guidance for New Players

You do not need to invent a full backstory. Choose what sounds fun and discover the rest during play.

## Guidance for the DM

Everyone has already been hired by the spymaster/patron to rescue the captive noble. These cards are not meant to explain why the characters accepted this specific job. They explain who the characters are beyond the job.

Avoid flaws that sabotage the group. Good flaws create tension, comedy, hard choices, or memorable moments without preventing the party from cooperating.

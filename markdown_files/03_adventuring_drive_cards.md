# Adventuring Drive Cards

Pick one. This is why your character became an adventurer in the first place.

## Protect the Vulnerable
You adventure because ordinary people are too often ignored, robbed, displaced, or sacrificed by those with power. You go where guards will not and stand where someone needs a shield.

**Question:** Who were you unable to protect once?

---

## Prove Yourself
You adventure because someone underestimated you, dismissed you, or decided your future for you. Every danger survived is proof that they were wrong.

**Question:** Who do you most want to prove wrong?

---

## Seek Freedom
You adventure because ordinary life, family expectations, debts, old masters, laws, or promises felt like a cage. The road is dangerous, but at least it is yours.

**Question:** What are you afraid might drag you back?

---

## Chase Wonder
You adventure because the world is vast and strange. Ancient ruins, impossible magic, forgotten gods, talking beasts, cursed mirrors, floating towers — you want to see it all.

**Question:** What impossible thing do you most want to witness?

---

## Find Redemption
You once failed, harmed someone, served the wrong cause, abandoned someone, or looked away when courage was needed. Adventuring is how you try to become someone better.

**Question:** What mistake are you trying to make right?

---

## Build a Legend
You want your name to matter. Maybe you crave glory, songs, titles, wealth, respect, or a story no one can ignore.

**Question:** What do you want people to say about you someday?

---

## Uncover the Truth
You believe the world is full of secrets: buried histories, hidden crimes, false religions, sealed ruins, corrupt institutions, or forgotten magic. Answers are rarely found somewhere safe.

**Question:** What mystery first set you on the road?

---

## Master Your Power
You have a gift, curse, oath, pact, discipline, rage, talent, bloodline, or technique that you do not fully understand. Adventuring forces you to test it.

**Question:** What scares you about your own abilities?

---

## Serve a Higher Calling
You believe your life has a purpose beyond personal comfort. That calling might come from a god, an oath, an ancestor, a prophecy, a philosophy, or a promise.

**Question:** What would make you question your calling?

---

## Escape Boredom
You tried normal life. It was unbearable. You adventure because danger, travel, and strange companions are better than sitting still and becoming predictable.

**Question:** What ordinary life did you run away from?

---

## Restore What Was Lost
Something was taken from you: a home, family, title, memory, reputation, sacred place, homeland, magic item, or future. You adventure to reclaim it.

**Question:** What was taken, and who do you blame?

---

## Help Your People
You adventure on behalf of a village, family, temple, clan, guild, monastery, caravan, neighborhood, or community. Your victories are not only for you.

**Question:** Who is waiting for you to come back successful?

---

## Pay a Debt
Someone saved you, trained you, spared you, healed you, or gave you a second chance. You adventure because you owe a debt to a person, community, god, or the world itself.

**Question:** What would finally make the debt feel paid?

---

## Get Rich Enough to Matter
You know coin cannot solve everything, but it solves a lot. Wealth means safety, influence, revenge, independence, or the ability to protect people no one else protects.

**Question:** What would you do if you became truly wealthy?

---

## Keep Moving from the Past
You are not adventuring toward something as much as away from something. The road gives you distance from grief, guilt, enemies, obligations, or memories you are not ready to face.

**Question:** What catches up with you in your dreams?

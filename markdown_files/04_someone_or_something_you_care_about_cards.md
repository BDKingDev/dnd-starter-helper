# Someone or Something You Care About Cards

Pick one concrete person, place, object, promise, group, pet, rival, or memory. This is not a general ideal like justice or freedom. It is something specific your character could name, carry, protect, miss, write to, return to, or worry about.

## A Younger Relative
Someone younger looks up to you and believes you are braver than you feel.

**Question:** What story have they heard about you that is only partly true?

---

## A Mentor
Someone trained you, protected you, challenged you, or gave you the first real chance of your life.

**Question:** What lesson from them do you still hear in your head?

---

## A Keepsake
You carry a small object that would look ordinary to anyone else: a ring, button, key, coin, ribbon, toy, letter, feather, holy symbol, tool, or broken blade.

**Question:** Who gave it to you, and why have you kept it?

---

## A Home You Left Behind
A village, street, shrine, farm, tavern, library, monastery, ship, camp, or hidden refuge still feels like home, even if you cannot return easily.

**Question:** What do you miss most about it?

---

## A Promise
You gave your word, swore an oath, accepted a duty, or made a vow beside a bed, grave, altar, fire, battlefield, or open road.

**Question:** Who would be disappointed if you broke it?

---

## A Community
A family, clan, guild, temple, school, caravan, crew, neighborhood, order, or found family depends on you in some way.

**Question:** What do they need from you most?

---

## A Rival
Someone always pushes you to be sharper, braver, stronger, kinder, or more dangerous. You may hate them, admire them, or both.

**Question:** What do they do better than you?

---

## A Lost Friend
Someone important is gone: missing, dead, transformed, imprisoned, exiled, or simply unreachable.

**Question:** What do you wish you had said to them?

---

## A Pet or Animal Companion
A dog, cat, hawk, rat, horse, mule, lizard, raven, goat, snake, beetle, or stranger creature has claimed part of your heart.

**Question:** What trouble does this animal cause?

---

## An Old Letter
You carry a letter, journal page, map note, recipe, confession, poem, wanted poster, legal notice, or unfinished message.

**Question:** Why have you never thrown it away?

---

## A Sacred Object
You carry or protect a holy symbol, ancestor token, ritual tool, relic, prayer bead, charm, mask, bell, candle, or marked stone.

**Question:** What would it mean if you lost it?

---

## A Place of Peace
There is one place where your character feels safe: a quiet pond, rooftop, shrine, workshop, hidden room, garden, library corner, or stretch of road.

**Question:** Who else knows about it?

---

## A Family Name
Your name carries weight: pride, shame, expectation, debt, fame, or danger.

**Question:** What do people assume when they hear your name?

---

## A Work in Progress
You are making, writing, mapping, composing, building, restoring, or studying something over time.

**Question:** What is still missing before it is complete?

---

## A Memory You Protect
There is a memory you refuse to let the world take from you. It may be joyful, painful, embarrassing, or sacred.

**Question:** What small detail keeps the memory alive?

# Flaw Cards

Pick one. A good flaw creates interesting trouble without derailing the group. Avoid flaws that require betraying, stealing from, or refusing to cooperate with the party.

## Reckless Protector
When someone helpless is threatened, you act before thinking.

**Question:** What kind of vulnerability makes you lose caution?

---

## Can't Resist a Dare
A challenge, insult, or raised eyebrow can push you into doing something foolish.

**Question:** What dare still embarrasses you?

---

## Too Curious
You ask one question too many, open things that should stay shut, and touch strange objects before checking them.

**Question:** What discovery taught you that curiosity has a price?

---

## Suspicious of Authority
Uniforms, titles, seals, and official voices put you on edge. You assume power is hiding something.

**Question:** Who in authority proved you right?

---

## Show-Off
You want people to see how impressive you are, especially when subtlety would be safer.

**Question:** Who are you secretly performing for?

---

## Hates Asking for Help
You would rather struggle, improvise, or suffer than admit you cannot handle something alone.

**Question:** When did asking for help go badly?

---

## Overexplains the Plan
You are clever, and unfortunately you know it. You sometimes keep talking when the plan needs action.

**Question:** Who usually cuts you off?

---

## Lies Too Easily
You lie even when the truth would work, then have to maintain the lie longer than expected.

**Question:** What harmless lie got out of control?

---

## Impatient
Waiting feels like failure. If a plan stalls, you want to kick it forward.

**Question:** What is the longest you have ever waited for something important?

---

## Afraid of Being Trapped
Locked doors, tight spaces, ropes, chains, cages, and dead ends can make you panic or lash out.

**Question:** When were you trapped before?

---

## Cannot Leave a Lock Alone
A locked door, chest, drawer, window, cabinet, diary, gate, or puzzle feels like a personal invitation.

**Question:** What was the worst thing you found after opening something?

---

## Takes Things Personally
Insults against your friends, home, faith, work, or skills get under your skin fast.

**Question:** What insult always works on you?

---

## Needs Proof
You struggle to act on trust, instinct, or faith alone. You want evidence, even when time is short.

**Question:** What happened the last time you believed someone too quickly?

---

## Soft Heart
You are vulnerable to sob stories, frightened children, wounded animals, and people who look like they need one more chance.

**Question:** Who took advantage of your kindness?

---

## Competitive
You turn small things into contests: who climbs faster, talks better, drinks more, spots danger first, or lands the final blow.

**Question:** What do you most hate losing at?

---

## Collector of Trouble
You pick up strange objects, rumors, enemies, favors, pets, debts, and curses without meaning to.

**Question:** What is the weirdest thing currently in your bag?

---

## Poor Sense of Danger
You understand danger in theory. In practice, you often assume things will probably work out.

**Question:** What obvious warning sign do you tend to ignore?

---

## Holds a Grudge
You remember every betrayal, insult, unpaid debt, and public embarrassment.

**Question:** What old grudge are you trying not to mention?

---

## Too Honest at the Wrong Time
You can lie, maybe, but sometimes the truth escapes your mouth at the worst possible moment.

**Question:** What truth once got you in trouble?

---

## Dramatic Timing
You wait for the perfect moment, entrance, speech, or reveal, even when immediate action would be smarter.

**Question:** What dramatic moment actually worked once?

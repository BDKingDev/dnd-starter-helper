# Printable Card Index

## Suggested Print Sets

For 4–6 players, print:

- 1 copy of `01_quick_start.md`
- 1 copy of `02_character_class_cards.md`
- 1 copy of `03_adventuring_drive_cards.md`
- 1 copy of `04_someone_or_something_you_care_about_cards.md`
- 1 copy of `05_flaw_cards.md`

Cut cards apart at the horizontal rules (`---`) if desired.

## Fastest Table Method

Lay out the character/class cards first. Once everyone has a class, lay out the other decks.

Each player chooses:

1. Character/Class
2. Adventuring Drive
3. Someone or Something You Care About
4. Flaw

Then ask each player to answer one question from any selected card.

## DM Tip

If a player freezes, offer two choices instead of the whole deck. For example:

- "Do you want to be driven by Protect the Vulnerable or Build a Legend?"
- "Do you care about a person or an object?"
- "Is your flaw more reckless, curious, or suspicious?"

# Source Notes

These cards are designed for beginner D&D one-shots.

They use a simplified version of D&D's character-personality structure. Official 5e character guidance presents personality traits, ideals, bonds, and flaws as categories for fleshing out a character. For new players, these cards collapse that structure into three plain-language choices:

- Adventuring Drive: why you became an adventurer.
- Someone or Something You Care About: the concrete person, place, object, promise, group, pet, rival, or memory that matters to you.
- Flaw: how you make things complicated.

They also follow one-shot pregen advice from Sly Flourish: keep characters direct, understandable, and easy to play at the table.
